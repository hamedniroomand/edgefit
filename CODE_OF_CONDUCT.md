# Code of Conduct

edgefit is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and contribute.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Critique code and data, not people.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and that they will explain why.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other behavior that would be unacceptable in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through [@hamedniroomand](https://github.com/hamedniroomand) on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block people who do not follow this code, for as long as needed.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
